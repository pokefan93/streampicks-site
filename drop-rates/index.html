Transparency

Pack drop rates.

Your pack tier and each card’s reveal position affect what you can pull. Check Drop Rates in the app for the current percentages for your account.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

See your current odds

Open Drop Rates from the pack purchase or opening screen in StreamPicks. Choose Standard or Bonus to see your tier, the odds for each reveal position, special-edition checks, and any saved Legendary guarantee.

There is no single per-card percentage table that applies to every player and every reveal. The in-app disclosure uses the current pack policy and your account’s progression.

Standard and Bonus packs

Each pack contains five cards. Standard credits open your current player tier. Bonus credits open the next tier, up to the highest tier. Both can be earned through play; some offers also let you buy credits with coins.

The final reveal is a creator drawn from the next pool, up to the highest pool. Earlier-pool cards remain eligible, and duplicates can appear. A higher pool does not guarantee a higher rarity.

The five rarities are Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Full Art. Your tier and the eligible card pool determine which rarities can appear.

Lucky Upgrade

Arm a Lucky Upgrade to make the final two reveals in your next eligible Standard or Bonus pack creators from the next pool. It does not guarantee Rare or better. If the pack becomes Mega Viral, your armed upgrade is saved for another pack.

Current rarity balance

The October 9 balance update lowers ordinary Epic, Legendary, and Full Art draw rates. Full Art has a 0.59% base weight in the Viral pool, down from 1%. Legendary weights are 4% in the Legendary pool and 7% in the Viral pool. Epic weights are 13%, 18%, and 22.5% in the Epic, Legendary, and Viral pools.

These are pool weights, not the chance for an entire pack. Reveal position, eligible cards, and genre restrictions affect each draw. Empty rarity buckets are excluded and the remaining weights are normalized. StreamPicks+ does not improve rarity odds.

Favorites and special pulls

Your Top 10 creators receive a small preference within the selected rarity when they are eligible. Favoriting a creator does not unlock a card outside your pool or increase that rarity’s base odds.

A pack can independently become Mega Viral: normally a 0.05% chance (1 in 2,000), or 0.25% (1 in 400) when a Viral-tier player opens a Bonus pack. Mega Viral includes a Full Art final reveal. Lucky Upgrades and Top 10 preferences do not increase its chance.

Special editions and alternate prints have separate eligibility rules. See the in-app disclosure for those checks and exclusions.

Saved guarantees

The current progression system does not have a recurring 30-pack Legendary or 100-pack Full Art guarantee. Eligible progress saved from the older system is honored as a one-time Legendary guarantee. If your account has one, its details appear in Drop Rates in the app.

Full Arts in the Season Vault

The Market can also offer specific Full Art cards for a guaranteed coin purchase. Full Art list prices are 6,300–8,700 coins before discounts, a 50% increase in the October 9 update. The offer shows the exact card and current price before checkout. These purchases do not use pack odds. Existing cards and completed purchases are retained.
