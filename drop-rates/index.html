Pity system

Legendary pity: if you open 30 packs without pulling a Legendary or higher, your next Standard pack guarantees one.

Full-art pity: if you open 100 packs without pulling a Full-art, your next Standard pack guarantees one.

Pity counters track per-pack-type and reset when the guarantee triggers. You can see your current counters in your in-app collection.

FAQ

Are pack contents truly randomized?

Yes. Each card in a pack is rolled server-side at the moment you open. The rates above are the actual probabilities used by the server.

Can I see what I'd get before opening?

No. Packs are sealed until opened. The published rates are the only preview.

Do rates change?

Sometimes — for new pack types, limited events, or balance updates. Any change is published here before it goes live, with a new "last updated" date.

What's the difference between Standard and Premium packs?

Premium packs have a guaranteed Epic-or-higher slot and slightly elevated chances at higher rarities. Premium packs are purchasable with in-game coins, earned through play, or included in Plus benefits.

Where does the coin currency come from?

Coins are earned through daily play, missions, ladder finishes, and season pass tiers. Coin packs are also available as optional in-app purchases. Coins do not change card rarity rates.
