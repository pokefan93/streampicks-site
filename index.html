Questions, answered

FAQ

Can I edit my decks on the website?

Yes. Open the player portal and sign in with the same account and sign-in method you use in the app. Open My decks to build, edit, and save your decks. Saved decks show up in the app.

Can I play battles in the web portal?

The web portal is for your collection, deck editing, and progress. Arena battles, Career, pack opening, and Leagues are in the mobile app.

Is StreamPicks free?

Yes. The game is free to download on the App Store and Google Play. Collect cards and earn rewards as you play. StreamPicks Plus is an optional subscription with more daily battles, Vault missions, the premium Season Pass track, and Plus customization. See the Plus comparison →

Where can I download it?

Download StreamPicks free from the App Store for iPhone or Google Play for Android. Both versions are available now.

Is this affiliated with YouTube or my favorite creator?

No. StreamPicks is an independent game — not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube, Twitch, any other platform, any creator, or any related brand. Creator names and likenesses are used for commentary and entertainment and remain the property of their owners. Card power levels and battle stats are fictional and balanced for gameplay. Creators can request removal anytime via our takedown page.

What ages is it for?

StreamPicks is for players ages 13 and up. Store content ratings can differ by platform and region; our minimum player age is 13.

What are the card-pack drop rates?

Card rarities are Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Full Art. Your pack tier progresses from Basic to Viral as you level up. Eligible cards and odds depend on your tier and pack type. See our pack details page for how it works, and open Drop Rates in the app for your current percentages and any saved guarantee.

How do I cancel Plus or delete my account?

Cancel Plus: in your Google Play (or App Store) subscription settings — instant, and access lasts through the billing period. Delete your account: in the app under Settings → Account → Delete Account, or via our data deletion page.

How do I get help?

Email support@streampicks.app or visit our support page. A real human reads every message.
