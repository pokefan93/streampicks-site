Questions, answered

FAQ

Is StreamPicks free?

Yes. The full game is free to download and free to play. StreamPicks Plus is an optional subscription that adds more daily play, more inventory, and cosmetics — never card-changing power. See the Plus comparison →

What ages is it for?

Rated 13 and up. Card pack mechanics are clearly disclosed and follow App Store and Google Play policies; full drop rates are published.

Is this affiliated with YouTube or my favorite creator?

No. StreamPicks is an independent fantasy game. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube, any creator, or any related brand. Creator names are used for entertainment purposes only, and scores are not derived from real YouTube metrics in accordance with the YouTube Data V3 API guidelines.

How are creators chosen for the game?

We add creators based on community feedback, breadth of genre coverage, and active audience metrics. New creators are added regularly. Want someone on the wall? Vote in the app, or pitch us at partnerships@streampicks.app.

What are the card pack drop rates?

Per-pack rarity percentages and the full pity-system rules are published on our drop rates page and inside the app. Both update whenever rates change.

How do I cancel StreamPicks Plus?

Through your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Cancellation is instant; your Plus access lasts through the end of the current billing period.

iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions.

How do I delete my account and data?

From inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Or visit our data deletion page for the email request flow. Deletion is permanent and removes your collection, leagues, and history.

Does the app collect my data?

Yes — account info, gameplay stats, and crash logs. We don't sell user data to third parties. Full details live in our privacy policy.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Add friends, form Teams, and challenge them directly in a private Arena queue. League invites work the same way — drop a code, draft together, sweat the standings.

How do I get help?

Email streampicksapp@gmail.com or visit our support page for what to include. A real human reads every message.
