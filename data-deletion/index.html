Data Deletion & Account Removal

How to delete your StreamPicks account and associated information.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

StreamPicks respects your right to control your personal data. This page explains how to delete your account and the information associated with it.

1. Delete your account in the app (fastest)

You can delete your own account directly from inside StreamPicks — no email needed:

  • open Settings → Account → Delete Account;
  • confirm by typing DELETE and re-signing in to verify it's you;
  • your account and data are then permanently removed.

Deletion is immediate and permanent: your collection, decks, leagues, teams, arena history, and virtual items cannot be recovered.

2. Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email support@streampicks.app and include:

  • the email address associated with your account;
  • your StreamPicks username (if available);
  • a statement requesting account deletion.

We verify your identity before deleting, then process the request.

3. What data will be deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • your account and authentication credentials;
  • your username, profile, and bio;
  • your card collection, decks, and virtual items;
  • your league, team, and arena activity tied to your account;
  • your preferences and settings.

After deletion, you will no longer be able to access your account.

4. Data that may be retained

Some limited data may be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons, including:

  • security and moderation logs
  • fraud-prevention records
  • aggregated gameplay statistics that do not identify individual users

We limit retained information to what is needed for these purposes. Security, fraud-prevention and legal records may still contain identifiers where necessary; aggregate statistics do not identify individual players.

5. Processing time

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are typically processed within 7–14 days, and we may contact you to verify your identity first.

6. Re-registering & data requests

After your account is deleted, you may create a new account using the same email address if you choose, but previous gameplay data will not be recoverable. To request a copy of your data instead of deleting it, use About → Privacy Requests in the app or email us.

7. Are you a creator?

This page is about deleting your player account. If you're a creator (or represent one) and want your name or likeness removed from the game, that's a separate request — use our creator takedown page.

8. Advertising and analytics information

The planned iOS 1.52.3 release uses Apple’s SKAdNetwork for install measurement, without TikTok or Meta advertising SDKs or an advertising-measurement opt-in. Apple’s attribution postbacks do not contain your StreamPicks account ID, email address or advertising identifier, so we cannot match those reports to your account to delete an individual install record.

Firebase analytics and diagnostics are separate from Apple’s install reports. For access or deletion requests involving analytics or information held by a service provider, email support@streampicks.app and tell us which service and platform are involved. Do not send passwords or payment details. We will verify your request and explain what we can locate or request for deletion. Deleting your StreamPicks account does not necessarily erase records held independently by a service provider or store.

You can also use the request channels in TikTok’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy for information those services hold. Store purchase records are controlled separately by Apple or Google. See our Privacy Policy for details.

9. Contact

If you have questions regarding account deletion or data privacy, please contact:

support@streampicks.app
