Support

Need a hand? We've got you.

Email's the fastest way to reach us. A real human on the team reads every message.

Contact

streampicksapp@gmail.com

We reply within 1–2 business days. For account or payment issues, we'll usually need a little more info from you (see below).

What to include in your message

To help us help you faster, include as much of this as you can:

  • The email address linked to your StreamPicks account.
  • Your StreamPicks username (if you know it).
  • The device + OS version you're on (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18.2).
  • A short description of what happened, and what you expected.
  • Screenshots or screen recordings if the issue is visual.
  • For purchases: the date of the transaction and the last 4 digits of the App Store / Play Store order ID.

If you're asking about a specific pack pull or Arena match, include the timestamp — it makes the support log much faster to find.

Common questions, faster answers

  • Pack drop rates: see our drop rates page.
  • Canceling Plus: manage subscriptions in your App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android) account.
  • Deleting your account: see the data deletion page.
  • Privacy and data handling: see our privacy policy.
  • Terms of service: see the terms.

Creator partnerships

If you're a creator and want to be in the game (or you represent one), email partnerships@streampicks.app. We'll get back to you.

Creator removal & takedown requests

StreamPicks is an independent game and isn't affiliated with any creator. If you're the creator depicted (or you represent them) and you want out of the game, we'll honor that.

File a formal request on our creator & rights-holder takedown page. It asks for your identity, your authority, and a signed attestation so we can act quickly and so random users can't request removals. Prefer email? Write streampicksapp@gmail.com with the same details.
