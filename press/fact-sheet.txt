SHORT DESCRIPTION
StreamPicks is a free creator card game for iPhone and Android. Collect and evolve cards across 12 genres, build a deck for Arena battles, climb a 50-stage solo Career, and draft fantasy Leagues with friends. Customize your cards in the Workshop and browse your collection on the web.

LONG DESCRIPTION
StreamPicks turns creator culture into a collectible card game. Open packs, find creators across 12 genres, and evolve your favorites into a 20-card Arena deck. Attacks, support cards, and Go Viral moments bring internet culture into the match. Take on live opponents, challenge friends, or follow a 50-stage solo Career through illustrated creator neighborhoods and boss battles. Beyond the Arena, draft creators into fantasy Leagues where real public performance drives the competition. Give cards a personal look in the Aura Workshop, discover surprises through other players’ Trending Picks, and bring your collection and decks to the web. Teams, missions, and a Season Pass give players more ways to play together and build their collections.

FEATURES
Arena: Build a 20-card deck, evolve your creators, and time your Go Viral moment. Battle matched opponents, challenge friends, or practice in Solo.
Career: Climb from your first stream toward 10 million subscribers across 50 stages. Meet rivals, take on bosses, and chase three-star clears.
Leagues: Draft creators with friends and compete across a season. Real public creator performance powers fantasy matchups, with trades, waivers, and playoffs.
Aura Workshop: Make an owned card your own with cosmetic intros, auras, and metal treatments. Build a look to match the rest of your collection.
Trending Picks: Browse recent packs from other players, watch the shuffle, and pick a face-down card to add a surprise to your collection.
Web collection: Sign in at streampicks.app/account to browse your collection, inspect full cards, and build or edit decks tied to your game account.
