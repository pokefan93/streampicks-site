SHORT DESCRIPTION
StreamPicks is a free creator card game for iPhone and Android. Collect and evolve cards across 12 genres, build a 20-card Arena deck, play a 50-stage solo Career, and draft fantasy Leagues with friends. Customize cards in the Aura Workshop and manage your collection and decks on the website.

LONG DESCRIPTION
StreamPicks is a collectible card game featuring internet creators. Players open packs, evolve cards, and build 20-card decks for the Arena. Battles use creator attacks, support cards, and Go Viral abilities. Players can face matched opponents, challenge friends, or play through a 50-stage solo Career with rivals and bosses.

In Leagues, friends draft creators and compete based on their public YouTube view growth. The Aura Workshop offers cosmetic changes to cards, including intros, auras, and metal finishes. Trending Picks lets players choose a card from another player's recent pack. Players can also join Teams, complete missions, and browse their collection or edit decks on the website.

FEATURES
Arena: Build a 20-card deck and battle matched opponents or friends. Evolve creators during a match, use support cards, and activate Go Viral abilities. Solo mode is also available.
Career: Play through 50 stages, face rivals and bosses, and work toward three stars on each stage.
Leagues: Draft creators with friends. Their public YouTube view growth determines fantasy scores across a season, with trades, waivers, and playoffs.
Aura Workshop: Customize cards you own with cosmetic intros, auras, and metal finishes.
Trending Picks: Choose a recent pack opened by another player, watch the cards shuffle, then pick one face-down card.
Web collection: Sign in on the website to view your collection, inspect cards, and create or edit decks for your game account.
