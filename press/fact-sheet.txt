SHORT DESCRIPTION (about 50 words)
StreamPicks is a free mobile collectible card game where every card is an
internet content creator and the attacks are creator culture: Go Viral,
Go Live, Demonetized. Collect 1,000+ creators across 12 genres, battle 1v1
in the Arena, and draft a fantasy league with friends.

LONG DESCRIPTION
StreamPicks is a trading card game about the internet itself, built
entirely by one person. Every card is a content creator, and the moves
are creator culture turned into game mechanics: Go Viral, Go Live,
Demonetized, Callout Culture.

All 1,000+ cards were statted and balanced by hand. The holographic foils
are real-time GPU shaders that shift as you tilt the phone, and pack
opening is tuned frame by frame.

Players battle 1v1 in the Arena against friends, matched opponents, or a
50-stage solo Career. In Leagues, friends draft creators and score points
from real public YouTube view momentum, like fantasy football for
YouTube. Monthly seasons, a Season Pass, and Teams round it out.

Every card can be earned through play. Packs come in six rarities with
published drop rates and pity timers, and StreamPicks+ adds convenience
and cosmetics, never wins.

FEATURES
- Arena: fast 1v1 card battles with PvP matchmaking, friend battles,
  Solo play, and a 50-stage Career
- Leagues: season-long fantasy drafts scored on creators' public
  YouTube view momentum
- Packs: six rarities, real-time holographic foils, published drop
  rates, and pity timers
- Teams, monthly seasons, and a Season Pass
- Every card earnable through play; no pay-to-win
